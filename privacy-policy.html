IB Civil Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the IB Civil Android application, developed by InfoBridgeIndia, handles information when you use the App.

Effective Date: 14 August 2026

IB Civil is a civil engineering and quantity surveying (QS) productivity application built for construction professionals. This policy describes what information the App handles, how it is stored, and the choices available to you. If any term here is unclear, please contact us using the details in the "Contact Us" section below.

Information We Collect

IB Civil is designed to work primarily on your device. Depending on how you use the App, the following categories of information may be involved:

  • Account credentials (email and password) used to sign in through Firebase Authentication.
  • Construction project information you create in the App, such as quantity surveying (QS) inputs, calculations, and generated project details.
  • Construction drawings or PDF files you choose to open or process in the App.
  • Photographs you choose to capture with your camera or select from your gallery to attach to a project, such as work-progress photos.

Each of these is described in more detail below.

Local Project Data

Construction project information, QS inputs, calculations, project data, and generated project information created in IB Civil are primarily processed and stored locally on your device. InfoBridgeIndia does not operate a cloud database that automatically collects or stores your construction project data. This means your project data generally stays on your device unless you choose to save, export, or share it elsewhere, as described under "Saving, Sharing and External Services" below.

Drawings, PDFs and OCR

IB Civil lets you select construction drawings or PDF files for processing within the App. Where supported, drawing and PDF analysis, including OCR (optical character recognition), is performed on-device / locally on your phone or tablet. We do not automatically upload the drawings or PDF files you select to InfoBridgeIndia cloud storage.

Camera and Photos

You can use your device's camera or gallery to attach work-progress or project photographs within the App. These photographs are stored as part of your local project data on your device. Project photographs are not automatically uploaded to any InfoBridgeIndia cloud server.

Saving, Sharing and External Services

IB Civil allows you to save generated reports and PDFs to your device. You may also choose to manually share or export files, such as reports, PDFs, drawings, or photos, to external apps or services of your choice, for example Google Drive, email, or messaging apps. This sharing is initiated by you, and once a file is shared with an external app or service, its handling is governed by that service's own privacy policy and terms.

IB Civil does not automatically upload drawings, reports, project photos, or construction project data to InfoBridgeIndia cloud storage.

Firebase Authentication

IB Civil uses Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google, to manage sign-in using your email address and password. Your password is handled directly by Firebase Authentication and is not stored or used within IB Civil's local construction project database. For details on how Firebase / Google processes authentication data, please refer to Google's own privacy documentation.

Data Sharing

InfoBridgeIndia does not sell your personal information. We do not share your construction project data, drawings, reports, or photographs with third parties, except where you choose to export or share files yourself to an external app or service, where required to operate core services such as Firebase Authentication, or where required by applicable law.

Data Security

We aim to use reasonable practices to help protect information handled by IB Civil, including relying on Firebase Authentication's built-in security measures for sign-in. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your device and account credentials secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored project data generally remains on your device until you delete it within the App, clear the App's data through your device settings, or uninstall the App, subject to the App's actual local-storage behavior. Because most project data is stored locally, InfoBridgeIndia does not hold a central copy of this data that we can delete on your behalf. We recommend maintaining your own backups of important project files, drawings, reports, and photos, since locally stored data may be lost if your device is damaged, lost, reset, or if the App is uninstalled.

Children's Privacy

IB Civil is a civil engineering and quantity surveying productivity application intended for construction professionals and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or applicable requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of IB Civil after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
