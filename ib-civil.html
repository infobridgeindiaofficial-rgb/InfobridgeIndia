Setting Out → Excavation → PCC → Foundation RCC → Pedestal / Columns → Backfilling → Plinth Beam → Under-Floor Work
1
Setting Out

Define the starting dimensions and layout of the structure.

2
Excavation

Use project dimensions to calculate excavation quantities.

3
PCC

Calculate PCC requirements for foundation preparation.

4
Foundation RCC

Manage reinforced concrete quantities for foundation elements.

5
Pedestal / Columns

6
Backfilling

Calculate filling requirements after foundation work.

7
Plinth Beam

Handle plinth-level structural quantities.

8
Under-Floor

Continue quantities required below finished floor level.
